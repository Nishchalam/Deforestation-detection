How to read this

  • Seasons. Each year is a composite of the clear Sentinel-2 images from the months you chose (June to September by default), not a single date, so what you see is a seasonal picture.
  • Forest is the model's forest mask for that year.
  • Loss (model) is forest in the start year that is not forest in the end year, from the change model.
  • Hansen is the Global Forest Change reference: tree cover 2000 of at least 30% with a recorded loss year in the chosen range. Hansen records loss by calendar year, and the range counts the years after the start year up to and including the end year (start+1 … end). It is a reference, not ground truth.
  • Agreement (F1, IoU, precision, recall) compares the model's loss pixels with Hansen's. Neither is ground truth, so differences can come from either source.
